TinkerBuddy

TinkerBuddy for schools and IT

For teachers, tech staff and privacy reviewers. TinkerBuddy is not made by Autodesk. Autodesk does not endorse it. Tinkercad is a trademark of Autodesk, Inc.

TinkerBuddy is a Chrome and Microsoft Edge add-on. It coaches students aged about 8 to 14 while they design in Tinkercad. It plans the project with them, points at the right button and checks each step. It cannot click, drag, type or change anything in the design. The student does the work.

It is made by Catalyst AI Consulting Inc., a New York company. You can email us at privacy@tinkerbuddy.app.

Install it for your students

On school Chromebooks, students usually can't add add-ons themselves. So IT installs TinkerBuddy for them. Add-on IDs:

  • Chrome Web Store: listed here once the Chrome Web Store approves TinkerBuddy; until then, ask us
  • Microsoft Edge Add-ons: listed here once Microsoft Edge Add-ons approves TinkerBuddy; until then, ask us

Google Admin console (Chromebooks and managed Chrome)

  1. Go to Devices → Chrome → Apps & extensions → Users & browsers. Choose the unit for your students (and teachers, so they can try it).
  2. Add a Chrome app or extension by ID, enter the Chrome Web Store ID above, and choose From the Chrome Web Store.
  3. Set the installation policy to Force install (or Force install + pin to browser toolbar). Save.

Does your domain block add-ons by the permissions they ask for? The list TinkerBuddy asks for is below. If student accounts are set as under 18, Google's third-party access settings may also need TinkerBuddy allowed.

Microsoft Edge (Intune or Group Policy)

Add this value to the ExtensionInstallForcelist policy (Intune: Settings catalog → Microsoft Edge → Extensions → "Control which extensions are installed silently"):

<Edge Add-ons ID>;https://edge.microsoft.com/extensionwebstorebase/v1/crx

Google Chrome on Windows or Mac (Group Policy, Intune or a configuration profile)

Add this value to Chrome's ExtensionInstallForcelist policy:

<Chrome Web Store ID>;https://clients2.google.com/service/update2/crx

TinkerBuddy needs Chrome or Edge version 120 or later, on a laptop or desktop. Tablets and phones aren't supported.

Running a class

  1. The teacher creates a class on tinkerbuddy.app with their school email. They get a class code and a private dashboard.
  2. Students open TinkerBuddy's Settings and type the class code. Only then does their progress reach the teacher's dashboard.
  3. The teacher sets the switches: daily AI limits, voice, workspace pictures, or a pause on AI answers for everyone.

New classes get a free 6-week pilot for up to 35 students, 20 AI answers per student per day. The pilot never turns into a paid plan by itself. After that, the Teacher plan is $299 a year per teacher, covering all their classes (up to 150 students). Schools and districts: by invoice, about $5 per student a year. Purchase orders welcome.

For daily limits, a day runs from midnight to midnight, New York time. Automatic step checks don't count toward a student's daily answers.

Student privacy law

  • Who should approve it. Please have the school or district approve TinkerBuddy, not just one teacher. A teacher who signs up confirms the school allows it.
  • COPPA. A school may agree for parents, for school use only. This follows the FTC's COPPA guidance for schools. We use student data only to give TinkerBuddy to the school, never for any other business purpose. The school can see and delete it at any time. Our privacy policy is the notice the school can share with parents.
  • FERPA. We act as a "school official" with a real educational need. The school stays in direct control of the data. We use it only for the school's purpose. We don't pass it on, and we delete it when the school asks.
  • California. We follow SOPIPA. That means no targeted ads, no profiles beyond school use, and no sales. We keep data safe and delete it when the school asks. Our agreement has the terms that AB 1584 asks for.
  • Illinois. We sign the written agreement that SOPPA requires. The list of companies that process data is in the table above. We tell the school of any breach well within SOPPA's 30 days.
  • Other states. We sign your state's or district's data privacy agreement. Ask us for our security summary and our model agreement.

AI and safety

  • Answers come from Anthropic's Claude through our server. Anthropic does not train its AI on this data. We don't store questions, answers or pictures.
  • TinkerBuddy tells students it is an AI program, not a person.
  • It only answers about building in Tinkercad. It never does the design for the student. It refuses unsafe requests. If a student says they are hurt, scared or bullied, it kindly tells them to talk to a trusted adult. We test this before every release.
  • AI can make mistakes. Students should use it with a teacher nearby.
