How it works

Their idea. Their clicks. A coach at their side.

Kids learn CAD by building things they care about. TinkerBuddy turns “I want a rocket” into a plan, then coaches one small step at a time.

1 · Plan

Plans the project with them

Breaks their idea into 3–6 parts, like “body”, “fins”, “window”, and shows “Part 2 of 5” as they go.

2 · Point

Rings the exact button

An amber ring shows which Tinkercad button to press next, and a short line says why it helps their build.

3 · Check

Notices when it’s done

A green tick when the step worked, the next step straight away, and kind help when something goes wrong.

It can’t take over.
TinkerBuddy can’t click, drag or change their design. If they ask it to build it for them, it says it’s their creation and offers the first small step.

Questions

Does it do the design for my child?

No. It can’t click, drag or change anything in Tinkercad. It plans with them, points at the right button and checks the step. They do the work and keep the credit.

What does it cost?

Families: free for up to 2 children, or the Family plan at $39 a year for up to 5. Classes: a free 6-week pilot, then the Classroom plan at $199 a year per teacher. Schools: by invoice. Paid plans are always your choice; we never move you onto one. See Pricing.

What do I need?

A free Tinkercad account and Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a laptop or desktop. Tablets and phones aren’t supported.

Which AI does it use, and is my child’s data used to train it?

Answers come from Anthropic’s Claude, through our own server. Anthropic doesn’t use this data to train its models, and we don’t store the questions, answers or pictures. TinkerBuddy tells children it’s an AI program, not a person.

Can my school use it?

Yes. Create a class, give students the class code and set the switches you want (AI limits, voice, pictures). We’ll sign your district’s student data privacy agreement on request: write to us at the address in the privacy policy.

Is this made by Tinkercad or Autodesk?

No. TinkerBuddy is an independent product that works alongside Tinkercad. Tinkercad is a trademark of Autodesk, Inc.
