Using TinkerBuddy safely

Last updated 2026-11-27.

For kids

  • TinkerBuddy is an AI helper. It is a computer program, not a person. It can't see you or hear you unless you press the mic.
  • It can be wrong. If a tip doesn't work or seems odd, try it a different way, or ask a grown-up.
  • Keep your details to yourself. Don't type your full name, where you live, your school, a phone number or a password. TinkerBuddy only needs to know about your design.
  • If something feels wrong, say so. If TinkerBuddy says something that is wrong, confusing or not OK, press Report this answer. Then tell a grown-up.
  • 3D printers get hot. Ask a grown-up before you print. Don't touch the nozzle or the bed. Printed parts and filament are not food.
  • If you are hurt, scared or someone makes you feel unsafe, stop and tell a grown-up you trust. TinkerBuddy can't help in an emergency. In the US, call 911.
  • Take breaks. Stand up, stretch and look at something far away now and then.

For parents and teachers

  • You are in charge. AI answers only start after a child types the family or class code you give them. Before that, only the built-in lessons work.
  • Your dashboard has the switches. You can pause AI answers, lower daily limits, and turn off pictures of the workspace and voice. You can delete your family or class at any time.
  • What TinkerBuddy is told to do. It only helps with designs in Tinkercad. It doesn't click or change the design: the child does every step. It won't help design weapons. If a child types personal details, it doesn't repeat them and reminds them not to share. If a child says they are hurt, scared or being asked to keep secrets, it answers kindly and tells them to talk to a trusted adult. If someone is hurt near a printer, it tells them to step away and get an adult.
  • It is still AI. It can be wrong or confusing, and no filter is perfect. Stay nearby, especially when a 3D printer is running.
  • Reports reach a person. We read every reported reply. If it is marked Not OK for kids, we get an alert right away. We keep reports for 90 days.
  • Talk about it. A good chat with your child or class: what an AI helper is, why it can be wrong, and why we don't share personal details online.
  • Questions or worries? Write to privacy@tinkerbuddy.app. Our privacy policy explains what we collect, and our terms explain the rules.
