TinkerBuddy privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-27. TinkerBuddy is not made by Autodesk. Autodesk does not endorse it. Tinkercad is a trademark of Autodesk, Inc.

TinkerBuddy is a browser add-on. It coaches children while they design in Tinkercad. It is made for children aged about 8 to 14. A parent or a teacher sets it up. So we collect as little as we can. We never sell data, and we never show ads.

This page is our notice to parents under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). It also sets out how long we keep each kind of data.

TinkerBuddy is made and run by Catalyst AI Consulting Inc., a New York company ("we"). We are the only company that runs TinkerBuddy.

The short version

  • Children don't make accounts. We never ask a child for a name, email or password.
  • Our server does not store questions, answers or pictures. We don't store what a child says either. The one exception is an answer someone reports.
  • AI answers only start after a parent or teacher gives the child a family or class code. Before that, only the built-in lessons work, and nothing is sent to our AI provider.
  • A parent or teacher sees progress only if the child types in their family or class code.
  • No ads. No tracking across websites. We never sell or rent data.
  • No one trains AI on children's data. Not us, and not our AI provider.
  • We only share data with the companies that help us run TinkerBuddy. They may use it only for that.

A child may type something personal in a question, such as a name. TinkerBuddy asks children not to. We don't store it. Our AI provider may not train on it, and deletes it within 30 days.

Voice

Voice is optional, and an adult can switch it off. If a child presses the talk button, the browser turns speech into text. Chrome sends the sound to Google to do this. Edge sends it to Microsoft. TinkerBuddy never gets the sound, only the text.

TinkerBuddy can also read answers aloud. Some browser voices send the answer text to Google or Microsoft to make the sound. The child's own words are not sent for this.

Families and classes (only with a code)

A parent or teacher can make a family or class. The child types its code into TinkerBuddy's Settings. Only then are learning moments sent to that adult's private dashboard.

A learning moment says that something happened, and when. For example: a question was asked, a hint was used, a plan was made, a step was checked, a part was finished, or a skill was practised. It never holds what the child typed or said, the answers, or pictures.

The adult can rename or remove a child. They can set daily AI limits, pause AI answers, and switch voice and pictures on or off. Both start off for classes and on for families.

The child can leave at any time in Settings. Sharing then stops at once. Moments already sent stay on the dashboard until they are 90 days old. They go sooner if the adult deletes the family or class. To delete one child's moments sooner, write to us.

What we get from parents and teachers

  • Email address. To send the link that finishes set-up, sign-in links, and messages about a paid plan. We never send marketing email. We never share it except with the companies listed below.
  • Dashboard name. You choose it, such as "The Lee family". You don't have to use a real name.
  • Payment. If you buy a plan, Stripe takes the payment. Stripe gets your card details, name, email and billing address. We never see your card number. We keep only Stripe's customer number, your plan and its dates.
  • Bot check. Sign-up and sign-in forms use Cloudflare Turnstile, so no one can make accounts or send emails by robot.
  • Email limits. We keep a scrambled code of your email and network address for 7 days, to limit how many emails one person can ask for.

You also get a private dashboard code. Keep it safe. Anyone who has it can see your dashboard.

Parents' consent and rights

Families. A parent sets up the family with an email address, confirms it from the email, and gives the child the code. By doing this, the parent agrees to what this page describes. The parent can stop at any time.

Schools. A school may agree for parents, for school use only. This is allowed under the FTC's guidance on COPPA. We then use the data only to give TinkerBuddy to that school. Parents can ask the school to see or delete their child's data. Or they can write to us.

Your rights. A parent can see what we hold about their child. A parent can have it deleted. A parent can stop us collecting more. To do this:

  • See learning moments on your dashboard.
  • Remove your child from the dashboard, or have them leave in Settings.
  • Delete the whole family or class from the dashboard. Its codes, nicknames, learning moments and reports are deleted at once. If a paid plan is still set to renew, cancel it first.
  • Remove TinkerBuddy from the browser. That deletes all it kept on that computer.
  • Or write to privacy@tinkerbuddy.app with the family or class code. We check that you are the parent first. We act within 30 days.

To stop all collection, remove TinkerBuddy from the browser. Leaving a family or class stops the sharing of learning moments only.

What stays on the computer

Settings, skills progress, project plans and the last few lines of chat stay in the browser on that computer. We can't see them. Use Settings → Reset progress, or remove TinkerBuddy, to delete them.

AI, and what TinkerBuddy tells children

TinkerBuddy uses AI. It tells children it is a computer helper, not a person. It is told to answer only about building in Tinkercad. It must never do the design for the child. It must refuse unsafe requests, such as weapons or personal details. If a child says they are hurt, scared or bullied, it tells them kindly to talk to a trusted adult. We test this before every release.

AI can make mistakes. An adult should stay close, mainly near a 3D printer. No person reads children's questions. We only read answers that someone reports.

Security

All traffic is encrypted, and so is stored data. Only our server can reach the database. AI keys stay on the server. Codes are stored only in scrambled form. We follow a written security plan and review it every year.

If someone gets into data they shouldn't, we will tell the adult who set up the family or class. For schools, we tell the school within 72 hours where we can. We never take more than 7 days.

Chrome Web Store

TinkerBuddy's use of data follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy. That includes its Limited Use rules. We use data for one thing only: coaching in Tinkercad.

Wherever you live

Anyone can ask us what we hold about them or their child. Anyone can ask us to delete it. We will not treat you worse for asking.
